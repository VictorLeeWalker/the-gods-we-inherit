The Gods We Inherit

by Victor Lee Walker

Inspired by Kaveh Akbar’s novel Martyr!, The Gods We Inherit explores the relationship between religious inheritance, identity, suffering, and the search for meaning. The poem considers what it means to inherit beliefs we did not choose and the struggle to reconcile those beliefs with our understanding of ourselves.

This interactive adaptation invites readers to participate in the creation of meaning. Throughout the poem, selected words can be changed, allowing language, imagery, and interpretation to shift with each choice. The religious text you select will also transform the accompanying scripture, instrumental music, and song title within the poem.

There is no single path through this experience. Each choice creates new possibilities for reading, questioning, and discovering meaning.
